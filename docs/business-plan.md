# Agent Git Lab Teams - business plan (draft)

## Executive summary
Agent Git Lab Teams helps bootcamp operators launch Git and GitHub learning paths in days, not months. The product combines a team onboarding portal with the Agent Git Lab learner app Joshua built during the Hult cohort. Starter pricing targets small cohorts; Pro adds analytics export and support.

## Problem and solution
Bootcamp leaders stitch Discord, Notion, and ad hoc workshops together. Learners stall before their first meaningful pull request. Agent Git Lab Teams gives operators a workspace, learner launch links, and qualified-action metrics without rebuilding PM, comms, or learning tooling.

## Market and competition
Target customers are coding bootcamps, university cohort programs, and internal academies training agent-first developers. Competitors include GitHub Education bundles, Codecademy for Teams, and custom LMS stacks. Our wedge is cohort-native workflows plus agent-assisted lesson paths already proven in production.

## Business model and pricing
- Starter: $49/month per cohort (up to 30 seats)
- Pro: $149/month per cohort (up to 120 seats, analytics export, priority support)
- Annual prepay: 2 months free

## Go-to-market
Founder-led outreach to bootcamp directors, demo days, and alumni communities. Initial pilots through Joshua's Hult cohort network and GitHub Education contacts.

## Unit economics (assumptions labeled)
- Assumed CAC (founder-led): $120 per paying cohort
- Assumed gross margin: 82% (hosting + support)
- Assumed LTV (12-month retention): $980 Starter / $1,790 Pro
- Payback target: under 2 months on Starter

## Product roadmap (12 months)
1. Q4 2026: Team portal MVP, learner launch links, metrics export
2. Q1 2027: Seat management, SSO for operators, Slack digest
3. Q2 2027: Lesson authoring lite, cohort PM snapshot integration
