# Customer interview script — Agent Git Lab Teams

**Founder:** RAVEN (@raven-dubgub)  
**Duration:** 15 minutes  
**Pass gate:** 5 external interviews with dated notes in `docs/market-research.md`

Cohort peers do **not** count as external validation.

---

## Before the call

- [ ] Confirm they operate or influence a cohort/bootcamp/academy (20+ seats preferred)
- [ ] Send calendar invite with Zoom/Meet link
- [ ] Open a blank notes section in `docs/market-research.md` for the matching slot
- [ ] Have https://venture-raven-dubgub.vercel.app/app ready if they want a live walkthrough

---

## Intro (1 min)

> "Hi, I'm RAVEN. I'm building Agent Git Lab Teams for bootcamp and cohort operators who need Git and GitHub fluency without rebuilding tooling from scratch."
>
> "This is research, not a sales call. I will take notes and may follow up with a pilot link later. Is that okay?"

---

## Discovery (10 min)

1. **Current onboarding:** How do you onboard learners to Git and GitHub today?
2. **Pain points:** Where do learners get stuck in the first two weeks?
3. **Tool stack:** What do you use today (Discord, Notion, LMS, GitHub Classroom, something custom)?
4. **Progress signals:** How do you know learners are actually progressing vs. just watching videos?
5. **Ideal outcome:** What would "good enough" look like for a lightweight team portal?

**Probe if vague:** "Can you walk me through the last cohort's week one?"

---

## Pricing probe (3 min)

6. **Price anchor:** Would $49/month per cohort (up to 30 seats) feel reasonable for a pilot?
7. **Switching cost:** What would make you switch from your current stack?

**Probe:** "Who signs off on a tool like this — you, director, finance?"

---

## Close (1 min)

8. **Follow-up:** Can I send a pilot link when ready?
9. **Intros:** Do you know one other operator I should talk to?

> "Thank you — this directly shapes what I ship next week."

---

## After the call (within 48 hours)

Fill the matching slot in `docs/market-research.md`:

| Field | What to capture |
|-------|-----------------|
| Date | YYYY-MM-DD |
| Contact | First name + last initial only in repo |
| Role / org | Segment (bootcamp director, TA lead, DevRel, etc.) |
| Quote | One verbatim sentence (redact PII) |
| Insight | What surprised you |
| Willingness to pilot | Y / N / Maybe |

---

## Segment targets (schedule 5)

| # | Segment | Example titles |
|---|---------|----------------|
| 1 | Bootcamp director | Program lead, head of education |
| 2 | University lab / TA lead | CS lab coordinator, teaching assistant lead |
| 3 | DevRel / tools startup | Developer education, community programs |

## Removed / non-qualifying

- Cohort peer interviews from the Hult program
- Anonymous placeholder quotes without dated contact notes
- Synthetic or AI-generated "interview summaries"
